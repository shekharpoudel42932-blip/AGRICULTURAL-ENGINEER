The dryer worked well in testing. Then the monsoon arrived early.

## Design for the worst week, not the average

Our model assumed average irradiance. Farmers, though, judge equipment by the week it fails them. A small biomass
heater, added late in the project, turned out to be the most valued feature.

## Ask about labour, not just yield

Loading trays twice a day was the hidden cost. Moving to wider trays on rails cut handling time in half.
