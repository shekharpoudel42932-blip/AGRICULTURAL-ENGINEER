Most irrigation schedules try to meet 100% of crop evapotranspiration. **Regulated deficit irrigation**
deliberately stays below that, and in many crops the yield loss is much smaller than the water saved.

## Stress at the right time

Crops are not equally sensitive across the season. In wheat, for example, water stress at flowering is costly,
while mild stress during vegetative growth often isn't.

<Callout title="Rule of thumb">
Protect flowering and early grain-fill. Most of the water savings come from the vegetative stage and late maturity.
</Callout>

## What you need to measure

1. Soil water depletion in the root zone
2. Reference evapotranspiration (ET₀)
3. A crop coefficient curve that is calibrated for your region

Without measurements, deficit irrigation is just under-irrigation.
