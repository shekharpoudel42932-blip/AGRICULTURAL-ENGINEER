title: Contour bunds vs. bench terraces on hill farms

Slope is the first filter. Below about 8%, contour bunds with a vegetative barrier usually do the job. Above about 15%,
you need bench terraces, and risers that can stand on their own.

Between 8% and 15%, it depends on soil depth and rainfall intensity. On shallow soils, cutting a terrace can expose
infertile subsoil, so we often choose **graded bunds plus grass strips** instead.

Whatever you build, maintain the outlets. Most terrace collapses I've seen started at a blocked drain.
