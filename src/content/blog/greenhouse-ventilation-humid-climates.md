In humid climates, evaporative cooling barely works, so ventilation is the main tool.

- Aim for a **vent area of at least 25%** of the floor area
- Put **ridge vents on the leeward side**, so wind pulls warm air out instead of pushing rain in
- Use **insect nets with a low pressure drop**, because a fine mesh can halve your effective vent area

A quick check with a CFD model before construction saved us from a dead-air zone in the middle span.
