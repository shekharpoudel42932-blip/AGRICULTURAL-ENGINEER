A tractor uses fuel most efficiently at about 70–85% of its rated load. An implement that's too small wastes engine
capacity, and one that's too large forces you into a lower gear, which wastes time.

Rule of thumb: **implement draft (kN) × speed (km/h) / 3.6 ≈ drawbar power (kW)**. Drawbar power is roughly
55–65% of engine power on tilled soil.

For a 35 hp (26 kW) tractor working at 5 km/h, aim for an implement draft of about 11–12 kN.
