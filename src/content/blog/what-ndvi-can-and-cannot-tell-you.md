NDVI maps look persuasive: green is good and red is bad. After 14 flights over a sugarcane estate, here's
what they actually told us.

## What it does well

- Shows **spatial patterns** you can't see from the ground, such as drainage lines, compaction and blocked laterals
- Tracks **change over time** when you keep the flight time and sun angle consistent

## What it can't do

- Tell water stress apart from nitrogen stress
- Work in dense canopies, where NDVI saturates (NDRE holds up much better)

Use the map to decide where to scout, then go and dig.
