title: "Grain storage: hermetic bags in practice"
description: Eight months of maize storage trials, and why moisture content matters more than the bag.
date: 2024-12-10
tags: [Post-harvest]
---

Hermetic bags work by starving insects of oxygen. It's an elegant idea, but it only works on dry grain.

Above about 13.5% moisture, respiration builds up heat and condensation inside the bag, and the grain spoils anyway.
The **salt-jar test** (dry salt, grain and a sealed jar: if the salt clumps, the grain is too wet) proved to be the
most useful thing we taught.
