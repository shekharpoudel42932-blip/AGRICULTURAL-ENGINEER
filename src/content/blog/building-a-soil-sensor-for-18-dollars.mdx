Cheap capacitive probes have a reputation for being noisy. Most of that reputation comes from poor sealing and
uncalibrated readings, not from the sensing principle itself.

## Seal everything

Moisture creeping into the PCB edge causes drift over weeks. A conformal coat plus heat-shrink fixed it.

<Callout title="Mistake I made twice" tone="warning">
Calibrating in a bucket of sieved soil. Field soil has structure, gravel and roots, so calibrate in situ.
</Callout>

```python
# simple two-point calibration
def vwc(raw, dry=3020, wet=1380):
    return max(0, min(1, (dry - raw) / (dry - wet)))
```
