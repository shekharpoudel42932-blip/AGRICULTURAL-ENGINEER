---
title: "Fertigation basics: venturi vs. dosing pump"
description: Venturi injectors are cheap and simple; dosing pumps are accurate. How to choose for a small farm.
date: 2025-07-15
tags: [Irrigation, Fertigation]
---

A **venturi** costs very little and has no moving parts, but it takes 20–30% of your pressure and its injection rate
drifts as pressure changes.

A **proportional dosing pump** keeps the ratio constant as flow changes and costs nothing in pressure. It costs
ten times more.

For a single-zone farm with stable pressure, a venturi with a flow meter on the suction side is fine. For multiple
zones or sensitive crops, pay for the dosing pump.
