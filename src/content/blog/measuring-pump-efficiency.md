---
title: Measuring pump efficiency in the field with a clamp meter
description: You don't need a lab to find out whether a pump is wasting energy. A clamp meter, a bucket and a pressure gauge will do.
date: 2025-11-02
tags: [Field notes, Renewable energy]
---

Wire-to-water efficiency tells you how much of the electricity actually ends up lifting water.

1. Measure **flow** with a bucket and stopwatch, or a clamp-on flow meter
2. Measure **head**: static lift plus the gauge pressure at the delivery (10 m per bar)
3. Measure **input power** with a clamp meter (voltage × current × power factor)

`Efficiency = (ρ g Q H) / P_in`. Small farm pumps should reach 40–55%. If you measure 25%, look for a worn impeller,
leaking foot valve or undersized suction pipe.
