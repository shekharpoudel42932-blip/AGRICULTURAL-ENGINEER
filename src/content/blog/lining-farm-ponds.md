In our 36 hill-farm ponds, every early failure came down to one of three things.

1. **Sharp subgrade.** Stones punctured the membrane, so we now sieve the subgrade and lay down a 5 cm sand bed.
2. **Weak anchoring.** Wind lifted the edges. A 30 × 30 cm anchor trench, backfilled and compacted, fixed it.
3. **UV and cattle.** An exposed membrane degrades and gets trampled. A 10 cm soil cover on the slopes doubles its life.

A 500-micron HDPE liner costs about the same as two seasons of lost dry-season vegetables.
