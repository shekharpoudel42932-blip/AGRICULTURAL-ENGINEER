As Agricultural Engineer at the Agriculture Knowledge Center, Banke, I was responsible for subsidised shallow
tube well (STW) irrigation schemes from start to finish:

- **Design and estimation** of each scheme.
- **Work completion reports** once each scheme was built.
- **Equipment distribution**, helping get subsidised equipment to farmers.

I also took part in farmer trainings on farm machinery and irrigation practices.
