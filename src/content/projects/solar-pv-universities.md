## Context

Nepalese universities rely on diesel generators during power outages, which is costly and polluting.

## Study

My undergraduate research compared fixed-tilt and seasonally adjusted tilt rooftop PV systems on technical,
economic and environmental grounds.

## Findings

Institutional rooftop PV can cost-effectively move a campus toward energy independence. It reduces dependence on
diesel generators during outages and cuts emissions. The findings scale to universities and similar
institutions in other developing countries.
